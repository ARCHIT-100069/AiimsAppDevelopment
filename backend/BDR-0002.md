# BDR-0002 — Module boundaries & communication (interface + events only)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Principal Engineer · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Bounded contexts (Blueprint §7) and per-context DB schemas (DDR 0004) only hold if modules cannot reach
into each other. Boundary erosion is the classic path to an unmaintainable monolith.

## Decision
A module communicates with another **only** via (a) the other's **public interface** or (b) its **domain
events**. No importing another module's internal services; no querying another module's tables. Enforced by
ESLint import-boundary rules + per-schema DB grants (DDR 0004).

## Alternatives Considered
- **Shared service layer / direct cross-module calls** — tight coupling, erodes contexts. Rejected.
- **Microservices now** — premature (ADR 0001). Rejected; boundaries prepare a clean future split.

## Trade-offs
Some duplication and event indirection vs direct calls; preserves modularity and the extraction path.

## Consequences
Contexts stay independent; the monolith→services split (ADR 0001) becomes mechanical.

## Dependencies
Blueprint §7, DDR 0004, BDR-0005 (events).

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Medium.
