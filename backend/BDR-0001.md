# BDR-0001 — Layered module architecture (controller/application/domain/repository)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Principal Engineer · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
A decade-long healthcare platform needs business rules that stay testable and don't rot into controllers
or ORM calls. NestJS gives structure; we must define the layering inside each module.

## Decision
Four layers per module, dependency-inward: **controller (HTTP)** → **application service (use-case +
transaction boundary)** → **domain (aggregates/value-objects/domain services; invariants)** ← **ports** ←
**infrastructure (repository/adapter impls)**. Domain is framework- and IO-free.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Anemic services + ORM everywhere** — business rules scatter; untestable. Rejected.
- **Full hexagonal/clean for every trivial CRUD** — overhead where not needed; we apply layering
  proportionally but keep the domain boundary firm.

## Trade-offs
More files/indirection per feature; buys testability, portability (ADR 0011 fallback), and clear ownership.

## Consequences
Business logic lives in the domain; IO at the edges; pure unit tests possible.
