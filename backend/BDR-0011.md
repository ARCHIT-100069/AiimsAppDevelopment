# BDR-0011 — Configuration & secrets management

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Principal Engineer · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Misconfiguration and leaked secrets are common failure/breach sources; config must be typed, validated, and
secret-safe (SDR-0006, ADR 0010).

## Decision
**Typed config** (`packages/config`) parsed and **Zod-validated at boot** (fail-fast). **Secrets** injected
from AWS Secrets Manager at runtime (SDR-0006) — never in files/images/repo. **Feature flags**
(`FeatureFlagModule`) gate mock↔real integrations and dark-launch (ADR 0010), resolvable per tenant.
Per-environment isolation; no prod secrets outside prod.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Unvalidated `process.env` access** — runtime surprises. Rejected.
- **Secrets in env files** — leak risk. Rejected (SDR-0006).

## Trade-offs
Boot-time schema + secret plumbing; buys fail-fast safety and no secret sprawl.

## Consequences
Invalid config fails immediately; secrets stay out of artifacts; safe progressive rollout.

## Dependencies
SDR-0006, ADR 0010, BDR-0008.

High. ## Reversibility Medium.
