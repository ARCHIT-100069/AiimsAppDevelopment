# BDR-0005 — Event flow via outbox dispatcher (in-process bus + BullMQ)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Principal Engineer · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Domain events must be delivered reliably to in-process reactions, async workers, and the audit log without
a dual-write problem (DDR 0007), while remaining operable for a small team (ADR 0001).

## Decision
Events are written to the **outbox** in the business tx; a **dispatcher** publishes to (a) the **in-process
event bus** (synchronous same-process handlers, e.g. read-model updates), (b) **BullMQ** (async work), and
(c) the **audit log**. Payloads follow versioned `event-schemas`. **At-least-once + idempotent consumers**.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Direct broker publish from services** — dual-write inconsistency. Rejected.
- **Kafka now** — operationally heavy for two founders (ADR 0001). Deferred to the scale path.

## Trade-offs
Dispatcher + idempotency effort; in-process (not distributed) eventing in the monolith era — honest and
sufficient now, with a clean broker migration later.

## Consequences
Reliable, replayable events; the monolith→services split reuses the same contracts.

## Dependencies
DDR 0007, ADR 0001, BDR-0004/0006/0007.

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Medium.

## Phase Classification
