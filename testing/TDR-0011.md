# TDR-0011 — Infrastructure, DR & backup/restore validation

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** QA/Eng Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
IaC, DR targets (IDR-0014), and backups are only trustworthy if tested — not assumed.

## Decision
- **Infra:** Terraform validate/plan + policy scans (tfsec/checkov/OPA) in CI; module tests; drift
  detection; post-deploy **smoke tests**.
- **DR:** **game-days** validating IDR-0014 targets (RPO ≤ 5 min; in-region RTO ≤ 30 min; cross-region RTO
  ≤ 2 h) — simulate region loss, fail over to ap-south-2, measure actuals.
- **Backup/restore:** periodic **restore drills** (PITR, snapshot, S3, audit-WORM integrity), verifying
  recoverability within RPO. A passing DR + restore drill is a **production exit criterion**.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Backups without restore drills** — false confidence. Rejected.
- **DR on paper only** — untested failover fails when needed. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
Game-days/drills take effort and coordination; the only way to trust recovery.

## Consequences
Recovery posture is proven, not assumed; RPO/RTO validated with real numbers.

## Dependencies
IDR-0011/0014, Ops phase (runbooks), TDR-0014.

## Confidence
Med-High. ## Reversibility Medium.

## Phase Classification
Structural Foundation (infra/smoke) + Production (DR game-days).
