# TDR-0009 — Performance, load/stress & chaos testing

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** QA/Eng Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
The platform must meet SLOs (IDR-0013), scale (IDR-0015), and degrade gracefully — especially that AI
failure never blocks care (AIDR-0014).

## Decision
- **Performance:** per-endpoint latency/throughput budgets tied to SLOs; hot-path regressions fail CI;
  query-plan checks for tenant-scoped indexes.
- **Load/stress:** k6/Locust against staging (peak + beyond-peak) validating autoscaling, RDS Proxy, rate
  limiting, queue backpressure; multi-tenant patterns.
- **Chaos:** game-day fault injection (AZ/DB/Redis/AI-plane/dependency) verifying graceful degradation;
  staging first, controlled prod game-days at maturity.

## Alternatives Considered
- **No perf/load gates** — surprises in prod. Rejected.
- **Chaos only in prod from day one** — risky before maturity. Phased.

## Trade-offs
Perf/load/chaos infra + baselines are effort; prevent outages and validate resilience.

## Consequences
SLOs verified; scaling proven; resilience/degradation validated.

## Dependencies
IDR-0013/0015, AIDR-0014, TDR-0011.

## Confidence
Med-High. ## Reversibility Medium.

## Phase Classification
Structural Foundation (perf/load) + Production (chaos maturity).
