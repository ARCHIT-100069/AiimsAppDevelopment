# TDR-0006 — RLS / multi-tenancy isolation gate (merge-blocking)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** QA/Eng Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Cross-tenant PHI leakage is the crown-jewel risk (SDR S-1). Isolation is enforced by RLS (DDR 0002) and
must be continuously proven — also guarding against Drizzle's silent RLS-policy drop (DDR 0011).

## Decision
A **merge-blocking cross-tenant isolation suite** (DB §17): seed tenants A/B; under A, every access pattern
against B (select/update/delete, direct id, join, jsonb, search) **returns 0/raises**; writing B's
`tenant_id` under A is **rejected**; run as **`aiims_app`** (FORCE RLS); **removing a policy fails the
suite**. Covers dedicated-DB routing and worker tenant-context. It is the highest-priority test in the repo.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Manual/periodic isolation checks** — misses regressions. Rejected (continuous gate).
- **App-layer assertions only** — doesn't exercise DB-enforced RLS. Rejected (real Postgres + app role).

## Trade-offs
Requires real-DB integration (Testcontainers) in CI; the cost is trivial vs a PHI breach.

## Consequences
Tenant isolation is provably enforced on every merge; the Drizzle footgun is caught automatically.

## Dependencies
DDR 0002/0011, DB §17, TDR-0003, IDR-0011.

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Very low (safety-critical).

## Phase Classification
Structural Foundation.

## Related Documents

## Review Trigger
Any isolation regression; tenancy model change.
