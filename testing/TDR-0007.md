# TDR-0007 — AI evaluation, safety & prompt-regression (release gate)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** QA/Eng Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
AI must never cross the Constitution (diagnosis/treatment) or leak PHI; prompt/model changes can silently
regress (AIDR-0012).

## Decision
A **release-blocking** eval suite per AI capability: **boundary/safety** (must refuse diagnosis/treatment),
**PHI-leak**, **prompt-injection red-team** (AIDR-0009), and **grounding/faithfulness + regression** sets.
Metrics (hallucination, refusal correctness, citation accuracy) tracked; HITL corrections feed evals. A
change failing safety evals **cannot ship**.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Manual AI review only** — misses regressions. Rejected.
- **Ship-then-monitor** — unacceptable for clinical-adjacent AI. Rejected (pre-release gate).

## Trade-offs
Building/maintaining eval suites is ongoing effort; the safety net for Constitutional compliance.

## Consequences
AI boundary/PHI/injection regressions are caught before release.

## Dependencies
AIDR-0012/0009, SDR-0004/0010, TDR-0012.

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Low.
