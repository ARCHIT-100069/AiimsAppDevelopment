# TDR-0001 — Testing philosophy + pyramid (shift-left, risk-based)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** QA/Eng Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
A clinical PHI platform needs high confidence without slow, brittle suites. We need a philosophy that
concentrates effort where failure is costliest.

## Decision
**Shift-left, risk-based testing** on a **pyramid**: many fast unit tests, fewer component/integration, few
E2E, plus **specialized safety gates** (RLS isolation, AI evals, security, DR) as blocking checks. Tests are
**quality gates** (pipeline blocks on them). Flaky tests are treated as bugs.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Ice-cream cone (mostly E2E)** — slow, brittle, expensive. Rejected.
- **Coverage-number-driven** — invites coverage theater. Rejected (risk-based).

## Trade-offs
Risk-weighting requires judgment on what's "critical"; yields fast feedback + high confidence where it matters.

## Consequences
Effort focuses on isolation/security/clinical correctness; fast local feedback; blocking gates.
