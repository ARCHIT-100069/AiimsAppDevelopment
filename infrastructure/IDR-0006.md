# IDR-0006 — Aurora PostgreSQL deployment (Multi-AZ, RDS Proxy, pgvector)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Cloud & Infra Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
The database is the system of record with RLS tenancy (DDR 0002) and pgvector RAG (AIDR-0006); it must be
HA, poolable, encrypted, and recoverable (ADR 0007).

## Decision
**Aurora PostgreSQL, Multi-AZ** (writer + ≥1 reader) in `ap-south-1`; **RDS Proxy** for pooling + failover
(supports the transaction-scoped `SET LOCAL` tenant context, DB §2). **Read replicas** for read models
(`aiims_readonly`); **pgvector** enabled. Encryption at rest (KMS), TLS-required, private subnets, **PITR**
+ automated backups + **cross-region snapshot copy to ap-south-2** (IDR-0014).

## Alternatives Considered
- **Self-managed Postgres on EC2** — ops/patching burden, weaker HA. Rejected.
- **Single-AZ** — no automatic failover. Rejected.
- **Non-Aurora RDS** — Aurora gives better HA/replica performance. Preferred.

## Trade-offs
Aurora costs more than single-node RDS; buys HA, fast replicas, and managed recovery.

## Consequences
HA, poolable, encrypted DB with PITR and cross-region DR; RAG-ready.

## Dependencies
DDR 0002, ADR 0007, AIDR-0006, IDR-0010/0014.

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Low.
