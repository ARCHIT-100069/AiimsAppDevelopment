# IDR-0004 — ECS Fargate compute topology

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Cloud & Infra Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
ADR 0001 chose a modular monolith on Fargate (not EKS) for operability by a small team. Infra must
realize this with independently scalable services.

## Decision
Separate **Fargate services** in private subnets: `api`, `workers`, `ai-plane` (flagged), `keycloak` —
each with a **least-privilege task role**, no SSH, service discovery via ALB/Cloud Map. Independent
autoscaling (IDR-0015). **No EKS/Kubernetes.**

## Alternatives Considered
- **EKS/Kubernetes** — cluster/node ops unstaffable now (ADR 0001). Rejected.
- **EC2 + self-managed containers** — patching/ops burden. Rejected.
- **Lambda for core** — poor fit for long-lived clinical workflows/DB pooling. Rejected (fine for isolated jobs).

## Trade-offs
Fargate costs more per vCPU than tuned EC2; removes cluster ops — the right trade for the team.

## Consequences
Operable containers, independent scaling, minimal ops surface.
