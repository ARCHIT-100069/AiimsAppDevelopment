# AIDR-0008 — PHI protection & redaction before model calls

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Models — especially external ones — must not receive raw PHI (SDR-0004, DPDP). Yet AI must operate on
useful context.

## Decision
**Redaction/tokenization at the gateway before any model call:** direct identifiers removed/tokenized,
de-tokenized only inside the trust boundary on return. **No raw PHI leaves the boundary** to an external
model. **Data minimization** (least context). **No PHI in prompts, logs, traces, or eval data**
(SDR-0010). PHI embeddings minimized, tenant-scoped (AIDR-0006), access-controlled; AI-derived stores honor
erasure/crypto-shred (ADR 0009).

## Alternatives Considered
- **Send full records to the model** — PHI exposure/DPDP violation. Rejected.
- **Rely on provider "privacy mode" alone** — insufficient; redact regardless. Rejected as sole control.

## Trade-offs
Redaction can reduce context richness and adds a step; required for privacy/compliance.

## Consequences
PHI stays inside the trust boundary; external models see only redacted/tokenized content.

## Dependencies
SDR-0004/0010, ADR 0009, AIDR-0002/0004.

High. ## Reversibility Low.
