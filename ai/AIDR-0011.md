# AIDR-0011 — AI audit logging + observability

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
AI decisions must be accountable and traceable for compliance/safety, without leaking PHI (DDR 0007,
SDR-0007/0010).

## Decision
**Every AI interaction is audited** via the outbox/append-only hash-chained log: actor, tenant, capability,
model + version, prompt-version, **redacted** input ref, output ref, route, human decision
(accepted/rejected), outcome — **no raw PHI**. **Observability:** OTel traces core→gateway→model; metrics
for latency/tokens/cost/cache/refusal/guardrail-trigger; alerts on cost spikes, hallucination-flag surges,
injection triggers, eval regressions.

## Alternatives Considered
- **No/loose AI logging** — no accountability, fails compliance. Rejected.
- **Log full prompts/outputs with PHI** — leak risk. Rejected (redacted refs).

## Trade-offs
Audit/telemetry overhead; essential for trust, compliance, and debugging.

## Consequences
Provable "what/why/who-accepted" trail; actionable AI telemetry without PHI.

## Dependencies
DDR 0007, SDR-0007/0010, AIDR-0002/0010.
