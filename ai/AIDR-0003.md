# AIDR-0003 — Isolated Python AI plane: deployment & scalability

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
ML/LLM tooling is Python; the AI workload must scale independently and never degrade core clinical APIs
(Blueprint §10, ADR 0001).

## Decision
Deploy AI as an **isolated Python (FastAPI) plane** (`apps/ai`) — its own Fargate service, versioned and
released independently, **stateless + horizontally scalable**. Embedding/generation run as **async workers**
(BullMQ) off the request path. CI runs **safety/eval gates** (AIDR-0012) pre-deploy; canary rollout; flags
decouple deploy from enablement. **The plane is optional** — the platform runs without it (fail-safe).

## Alternatives Considered
- **AI in the Node core** — wrong runtime for ML; couples scaling/failures. Rejected.
- **Third-party AI SaaS only** — less control over PHI/residency/audit. Rejected as the plane (may be a
  provider behind the gateway).

## Trade-offs
A second runtime/service to operate; isolates AI risk and scaling from core care flows.

## Consequences
AI scales and fails independently; core clinical APIs are unaffected by AI load/outage.

## Dependencies
Blueprint §10, ADR 0001, BDR-0007, AIDR-0002/0014.

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Medium.

## Phase Classification
