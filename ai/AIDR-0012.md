# AIDR-0012 — AI evaluation & safety gates

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Prompt/model changes can silently regress safety (e.g. start answering diagnostic questions) or leak PHI.
We need automated gates.

## Decision
**Eval harness per capability:** faithfulness/grounding, **boundary tests** (must refuse diagnosis/
treatment), PHI-leak tests, injection red-team suites, regression sets. **Evals gate releases** — a change
failing safety evals cannot ship. Human review of samples; HITL corrections (AIDR-0010) feed eval sets.
Tracks hallucination rate, refusal correctness, grounding accuracy over time.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Manual spot-checks only** — misses regressions at scale. Rejected.
- **Ship first, monitor later** — unacceptable for clinical-adjacent AI. Rejected (pre-release gates).

## Trade-offs
Building/maintaining eval suites is ongoing effort; the safety net for Constitutional compliance.

## Consequences
Safety/boundary regressions are caught before release; measurable AI quality.

## Dependencies
