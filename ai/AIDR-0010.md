# AIDR-0010 — Human-in-the-loop workflows (Constitution)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
The Constitution requires doctors to be the final decision-makers; AI outputs must never auto-apply to
clinical records.

## Decision
**Every AI output touching a clinical artifact is a suggestion** presented for clinician review; the
clinician edits/accepts/rejects and **signs** (sign is human-only). AI **never auto-commits** to a clinical
record. Outputs are labeled **"AI-generated — verify."** Acceptance/rejection is audited (AIDR-0011) and
feeds evaluation (AIDR-0012). Operational (non-clinical) AI still surfaces to a human actor.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Auto-apply AI output** — violates the Constitution; unsafe. Rejected.
- **Silent AI edits** — no accountability. Rejected (explicit, labeled, audited).

## Trade-offs
HITL adds a human step (by design); guarantees clinician control and accountability.

## Consequences
Doctors remain decision-makers; AI is assistive and auditable.
