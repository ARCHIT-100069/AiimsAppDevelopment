# AIDR-0005 — RAG + knowledge management (tenant-scoped, grounded)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Operational AI must be grounded in the organization's own data, tenant-isolated, and never present
external medical corpora as clinical truth (Constitution, DDR 0002).

## Decision
**RAG over the tenant's own operational data only**, **tenant-scoped** (RLS on embeddings, AIDR-0006).
Pipeline: chunk → embed → retrieve top-k (similarity + filters) → **ground the answer with citations** to
source records. Low-confidence/ungrounded → abstain/defer to human. Knowledge sources are registered,
permissioned, RBAC/RLS-respecting, and refreshed via the event stream (BDR-0005) with retained provenance.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Generate without retrieval** — hallucination risk, ungrounded. Rejected.
- **Cross-tenant / external medical KB as authority** — tenancy + Constitution violation. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
RAG infra (embedding/index/provenance) is real work; delivers grounded, auditable, tenant-safe answers.

## Consequences
Answers are grounded, cited, tenant-isolated, and refuse when unsupported.

## Dependencies
DDR 0002, BDR-0005, AIDR-0006/0008.
