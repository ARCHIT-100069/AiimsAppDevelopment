# AIDR-0001 — Operational-only AI boundary + governance + feature flags (Constitution)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
The AIIMS+ Constitution forbids AI diagnosis/treatment and makes doctors the final decision-makers. This
must be enforced by architecture, not just policy text.

## Decision
AI is **operational-only** with an **allow-list** of use-cases; anything else is disallowed by default.
Governance: each capability has an owner, HITL step, eval suite, and audit trail; prompts/models/sources
are versioned and reviewed; every capability sits behind a **feature flag with a kill switch**. Forbidden
categories (diagnosis, treatment/medication recommendation, autonomous clinical decisions, AI clinical
writes) are rejected at the gateway and tested by release-gating evals.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Policy-only guardrails** — unenforceable; models drift. Rejected (architecture enforcement).
- **Open-ended assistant** — risks crossing clinical lines. Rejected (allow-list).

## Trade-offs
An allow-list constrains flexibility and requires governance overhead — the intended safety property.

## Consequences
AI cannot, by construction, act as a clinician; new capabilities require deliberate sign-off.
