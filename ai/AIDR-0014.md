# AIDR-0014 — AI fail-safe & availability (off critical path)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** AI Systems Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
AI must never endanger care delivery through unavailability, latency, or low-quality output (Blueprint §28).

## Decision
**AI is never on the critical path of care.** If the AI plane is down/slow/low-confidence, **clinical
workflows continue** without it; UIs degrade gracefully. **Circuit breakers + timeouts + fallback routes**
(AIDR-0004) on provider failure; **low-confidence/ungrounded → abstain and defer to human**, never guess.
Guardrail failure ⇒ **block + audit**, not silent pass. The plane is optional infrastructure (AIDR-0003).

## Alternatives Considered
- **AI in the critical path (e.g. blocking documentation on AI)** — availability/safety risk. Rejected.
- **Best-effort guess on low confidence** — unsafe. Rejected (abstain/defer).

## Trade-offs
Fallbacks/abstention reduce AI "coverage"; guarantees care is never blocked or misled by AI.

## Consequences
Robust degradation; AI outages/quality issues never harm clinical operations.
