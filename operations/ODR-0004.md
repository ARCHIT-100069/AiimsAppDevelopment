# ODR-0004 — On-call strategy + escalation policies

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
A 24/7 clinical system needs reliable coverage without burning out a small team; escalation must be fast for
security/patient-safety.

## Decision
**24/7 rotation** (primary + secondary), humane load via automation + alert tuning; on-call has access,
runbooks, and authority to act. **Tiered escalation** (primary → secondary → eng lead → founders/CTO);
**security incidents** escalate to the Security Architect immediately, **patient-safety** fastest;
time-based auto-escalation if unacknowledged. **Alert quality is an SLO** — every page must be actionable.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Business-hours only** — unacceptable for clinical uptime. Rejected.
- **Everyone paged for everything** — fatigue, slow response. Rejected (tiered).

## Trade-offs
On-call burden on a small team; mitigated by automation, alert hygiene, and secondary support.

## Consequences
Reliable coverage; fast, appropriate escalation; sustainable load.
