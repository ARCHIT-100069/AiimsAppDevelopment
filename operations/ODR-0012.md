# ODR-0012 — DR operations, backup validation, game-days & business continuity

**Status:** Accepted (business RTO sign-off pending) · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead
**Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
DR targets (IDR-0014) and backups are only trustworthy if operated and exercised; hospitals must keep
functioning even if the platform is impaired.

## Decision
- **DR ops:** runbook for failover to `ap-south-2`; decision authority + comms; targets **RPO ≤ 5 min,
  in-region RTO ≤ 30 min, cross-region RTO ≤ 2 h**; **business RTO sign-off** is an operational readiness
  item (Ops owns confirming it).
- **Backup validation:** scheduled **restore drills** (PITR/snapshot/S3/audit-WORM) prove recoverability
  within RPO; failed drill = high-sev.
- **Game-days:** DR, component-loss, dependency-latency, security/breach, SEV1 drills — validate runbooks,
  alerts, MTTR.
- **BCP:** degraded-mode clinical procedures, manual fallbacks, tenant comms, SLA commitments; AI/notification
  outages never block care (AIDR-0014).

## Alternatives Considered
- **Untested DR/backups** — fail when needed. Rejected (drills + game-days).
- **No BCP** — hospitals stranded on outage. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
Drills/game-days cost time; the only way to trust recovery and continuity.

## Consequences
Proven recovery within targets; resilient operations; hospitals keep running in degraded mode.

## Dependencies
