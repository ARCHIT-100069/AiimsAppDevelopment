# ODR-0006 — Change & release management

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Production changes to a clinical PHI system must be controlled, traceable, and reversible without blocking
delivery velocity.

## Decision
**Change types:** standard (pre-approved/automated), normal (reviewed), emergency (expedited + retrospective
review); all IaC/PR-based (IDR-0012), auditable, reversible. **Release:** immutable image promotion through
envs; **release gates** (Testing §27) must be green; **feature flags decouple deploy from release** (ADR
0010). **Change-freeze windows** for high-risk periods; **no risky changes when error budget is exhausted**.

## Alternatives Considered
- **No change control** — untraceable, risky prod changes. Rejected.
- **Heavyweight CAB for every change** — kills velocity. Rejected (tiered change types).

## Trade-offs
Change discipline adds process; buys traceability, safety, and reversibility.

## Consequences
Controlled, auditable, reversible production evolution at a sustainable pace.

## Dependencies
IDR-0012/0015, TDR-0012, ADR 0010, ODR-0002 (error budget).
