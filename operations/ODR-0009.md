# ODR-0009 — Capacity, performance & cost monitoring

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Growth (tenants, data) must be planned; performance must stay within SLOs; cost — including AI tokens — must
be controlled for the business model.

## Decision
- **Capacity:** trend SLIs/saturation + tenant growth; autoscaling (IDR-0015) for short-term, planning for
  structural (read replicas, **observation partitioning** trigger DDR-0010, **dedicated-DB promotion**
  ADR-0002); load tests (TDR-0009) before large onboardings.
- **Performance:** continuous p50/p95/p99, slow-query/plan monitoring for tenant-scoped indexes; CI perf
  budgets.
- **Cost (FinOps):** per-tenant/service tags, budgets + anomaly alerts, AI token metering (AIDR-0013),
  Spot/Savings-Plan utilization; **unit economics** (cost per tenant/encounter) as a KPI.

## Alternatives Considered
- **React-only scaling** — outages/overspend from surprises. Rejected (plan + autoscale).
- **No cost governance** — margin erosion, esp. AI. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
Forecasting + FinOps effort; prevents outages and protects unit economics.

## Consequences
Predictable capacity, in-SLO performance, controlled and understood cost.

## Dependencies
IDR-0015, DDR-0010, ADR-0002, AIDR-0013, TDR-0009.
