# ODR-0005 — Runbooks & playbooks

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Under pressure, responders need tested, current procedures — not improvisation — especially for DB failover,
breach, and DR.

## Decision
**Versioned per-service runbooks** (deploy/rollback, DB failover/restore, Redis loss, AI-plane outage,
cert/secret rotation, tenant-isolation alert, audit-integrity failure, cost spike) linked from their alerts,
and **scenario playbooks** (SEV1 outage, PHI breach, DR failover, provider outage, data-restore). All in git
(GOVERNANCE §2 headers), **tested in game-days** (§25), reviewed after each incident.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Tribal knowledge / no runbooks** — slow, error-prone under stress. Rejected.
- **Write-once, never-tested docs** — stale, misleading. Rejected (game-day tested).

## Trade-offs
Authoring + upkeep effort; drastically improves MTTR and reduces error during incidents.

## Consequences
Fast, consistent, tested response; onboarding is easier.
