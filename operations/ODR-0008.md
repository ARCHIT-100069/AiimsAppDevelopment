# ODR-0008 — Dashboards + alert management

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Operators need clear visibility and **actionable, PHI-free** alerts (IDR-0013, SDR-0010); alert noise is a
reliability risk.

## Decision
**Dashboards:** service (SLO burn, RED/USE, queue depth, replica lag), business/flow (OPD health), security/
compliance (authz denials, isolation anomalies, audit-integrity, AI guardrails), and **cost** (per tenant/
service, incl. AI tokens) — **no PHI** anywhere. **Alerts:** symptom-based, tied to SLO burn + hard failures
(audit-integrity, isolation anomaly, DR/backup failure, security events); each links a runbook; **alert
hygiene** (noise/flap, MTTA/MTTR) tracked; paging vs ticket by severity.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Cause-based alert sprawl** — noisy, fatiguing. Rejected (symptom/SLO-based).
- **PHI on dashboards for "context"** — leak risk. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
Dashboard/alert curation is ongoing; delivers fast detection without fatigue.

## Consequences
Actionable visibility; low-noise, runbook-linked alerts; no PHI exposure.

## Dependencies
IDR-0013, SDR-0010, ODR-0002/0005.
