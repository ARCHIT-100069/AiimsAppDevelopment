# ODR-0013 — Operational readiness reviews + production readiness checklist

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Going to production (or onboarding a major tenant/feature) with PHI must clear an explicit, non-negotiable
bar (extends TDR-0014).

## Decision
- **ORR** before prod onboarding of a tenant/major feature: SLOs defined, dashboards + alerts wired,
  runbooks present + tested, on-call ready, capacity validated, security/compliance signed off, rollback
  proven — a **gate**, not a formality.
- **Production readiness checklist (superset of Testing §30):** all release/quality gates green; **isolation
  suite green**; **AI evals green**; **pen-test criticals closed**; **DR + restore drill passed** + **business
  RTO signed off**; SLOs/budgets/dashboards/alerts live; on-call/runbooks/escalation ready; audit verifier
  running; DPDP/ABDM go-live-scope duties operational; **offline-PHI device posture + LLM residency resolved**
  if enabled. **No red item → no go-live.**

## Alternatives Considered
- **"Feels ready" launch** — unsafe for clinical PHI. Rejected (explicit gate).

## Trade-offs
A hard gate can delay launch; the point is safety for patients and PHI.

## Consequences
Defensible, safety-first go-live and onboarding decisions.

## Dependencies
TDR-0014, all ODRs, open items (offline-PHI, LLM residency, business RTO).

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Low.
