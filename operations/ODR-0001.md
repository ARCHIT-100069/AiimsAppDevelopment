# ODR-0001 — Operational philosophy + SRE model

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
A clinical PHI platform must be run reliably by a small team; reliability is a patient-safety concern, and
toil must not overwhelm two founders.

## Decision
**Reliability-as-a-feature**, **you-build-it-you-run-it (lite)** with a lean SRE platform function,
**blameless** incident culture, and **error-budget-driven** velocity/stability balance. **Toil is measured
and automated away.** Operability (health checks, observability, runbooks) is designed-in. Model scales from
shared founder roles to dedicated SRE as the team grows.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Separate ops silo (throw-over-the-wall)** — slow, low ownership. Rejected.
- **No formal SRE practice** — reliability by luck; unacceptable for clinical. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
Engineers carry on-call/ownership; buys fast feedback and real reliability with a small team.

## Consequences
Clear ownership, automation-first ops, sustainable on-call, learning culture.
