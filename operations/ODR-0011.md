# ODR-0011 — Audit & compliance operations

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** SRE/Ops Lead + Compliance · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
Audit integrity and DPDP/ABDM duties are ongoing operational obligations, not just design properties
(DDR-0007, SDR-0007, ADR-0009).

## Decision
- **Audit ops:** **scheduled hash-chain integrity verifier** (failure = high-sev alert); WORM archive in the
  security account; access-to-audit is itself audited + least-privilege; retention per policy.
- **Compliance ops:** DPDP records-of-processing upkeep; **consent-manager operations** (Prod);
  **data-principal rights** handling (access/correction/erasure via retention engine, ADR-0009); ABDM
  obligations; **SDF duties** (DPO/DPIA/audits) if classified. Compliance calendar + evidence collection.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Assume audit integrity** — undetected tampering. Rejected (verifier).
- **Handle rights requests ad hoc** — non-compliant, slow. Rejected (defined ops).

## Trade-offs
Compliance operations are recurring work; required to remain lawful and audit-ready.

## Consequences
Provable audit integrity; timely, compliant handling of regulatory duties.

## Dependencies
DDR-0007, SDR-0007, ADR-0009, C-1/C-2 legal items.

## Confidence
High. ## Reversibility Medium.

## Phase Classification
Structural Foundation + Production (consent-manager, SDF duties).

## Related Documents
OPERATIONS-ARCHITECTURE §21/§22.

## Review Trigger
Audit-integrity failure; regulatory change; SDF classification.
