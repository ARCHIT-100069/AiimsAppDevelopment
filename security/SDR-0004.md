# SDR-0004 — PHI protection & field encryption (ratifies DDR-0006)

**Status:** Accepted · **Version:** 1.0 · **Owner:** Security Architect · **Date:** 2026-07-04

## Context
DDR-0006 proposed encrypting **direct identifiers only** (not all PHI) with per-record envelope keys +
blind indexes, and was left pending Security-phase sign-off. This SDR is that review.

## Decision
**Ratify DDR-0006.** Field-encrypt direct identifiers (`abha_number`, `phone`, `email`, Aadhaar refs) with
per-record data keys wrapped by a KMS CMK (SDR-0005); use keyed-HMAC **blind indexes** for exact-match
lookup. All other PHI relies on Aurora at-rest KMS + RLS and stays queryable. PHI excluded from logs,
metrics, traces, AI prompts, and non-prod data.

## Alternatives Considered
- Encrypt all PHI columns — breaks search/joins/indexes; unusable OLTP. Rejected.
- No field encryption (at-rest only) — cannot crypto-shred a single subject for erasure. Insufficient.
- In-DB pgcrypto with DB-held key — weaker key separation than KMS envelope. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
Encrypted identifiers need blind indexes and cannot be range-queried — acceptable for identifiers. Balances
DPDP erasure/crypto-shred against performance (blueprint goals).

## Consequences
Erasure honored via crypto-shred once retention expires (ADR 0009); operational queryability preserved.
Closes the DDR-0006 open item.

## Dependencies
DDR-0006, ADR-0009/DDR-0013, SDR-0005 (KMS).

## Confidence
High.

## Reversibility
Low — changing the encrypted-field set is a data migration.

## Phase Classification
Structural Foundation (columns/keys/blind indexes) + Production Implementation (shred engine, durations).

## Related Documents
DDR-0006, SECURITY-BLUEPRINT §10/§13.

## Review Trigger
New identifier types; regulatory change to erasure; crypto-agility upgrade.
