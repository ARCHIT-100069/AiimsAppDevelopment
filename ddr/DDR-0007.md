# DDR 0007 — Transactional outbox + append-only hash-chained audit

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-07-04 · **Confidence:** High · **Implements:** ADR 0005

## Context
Events must never be lost or emitted for rolled-back writes, and audit must be tamper-evident (ADR 0005,
Constitution, DPDP accountability).

## Decision
- **`platform.outbox`** — event row written in the **same transaction** as the business change; a
  dispatcher fans out to the in-process bus, BullMQ, and the audit log. Versioned `event_type`.
- **`audit.audit_log`** — insert-only, **hash-chained** (`row_hash = H(prev_hash ‖ canonical(row))`),
  capturing actor/action/resource/consent_ref/context, **no PHI payloads**. `UPDATE`/`DELETE` revoked from
  `aiims_app`.
- Both partitioned monthly; audit archived to S3 Object Lock (WORM) in Production; a verifier re-walks the
  chain.

## Alternatives considered
- **Publish to a broker directly from app code** — dual-write problem (DB commits, publish fails or vice
  versa). Rejected in favor of the outbox.
- **Mutable audit table** — not tamper-evident. Rejected.

## Trade-offs
Outbox adds a dispatcher and a little write amplification; hash-chaining adds compute on insert. Both are
mandatory for correctness/compliance and are cheap relative to the guarantee.

## Consequences
Exactly-once-ish delivery with idempotent consumers; provable audit integrity; the event contracts enable
the future monolith→services split (ADR 0001).
