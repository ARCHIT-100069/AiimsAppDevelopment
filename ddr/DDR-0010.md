# DDR 0010 — Time-range partitioning for high-volume tables

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-07-04 · **Confidence:** High · **Implements:** ADR 0001

## Context
Audit and outbox grow unbounded and are read by time window; some clinical tables (observations) will grow
very large. Unpartitioned, they degrade vacuum, index size, and archival.

## Decision
- **`audit.audit_log` and `platform.outbox`: range-partition by month from day one.** Old partitions are
  cheaply detached/dropped or archived to WORM.
- **`clinical.observation` (and later results/notes):** partitioning **designed-for now, enabled when
  volume warrants** — by month, or by tenant hash for very large tenants.
- Dedicated-DB promotion (ADR 0002) is the complementary lever for the biggest tenants.

## Alternatives considered
- **No partitioning** — unbounded growth, painful archival, bloated indexes. Rejected for audit/outbox.
- **Partition every table now** — premature complexity for low-volume tables. Rejected (enable on evidence).

## Trade-offs
Partitioning adds DDL/maintenance (partition creation automation) and constrains some unique/FK patterns to
include the partition key. Justified for the high-volume, time-scoped tables; deferred elsewhere.

## Consequences
Predictable performance and cheap retention/archival for audit/outbox; a clear, non-disruptive path to
partition observations when metrics cross the threshold (DATABASE-ARCHITECTURE §19.4).
