# ADR 0009 — Retention & erasure: engineering finalized, durations deferred for legal validation

**Status:** Proposed — **Deferred for Legal Validation** · **Date:** 2026-07-03 · **Confidence:** Medium
**Reversibility: Very low — it shapes the data model (ADR 0003).**
**Phase classification (ADR 0010):** Structural Foundation (per-record encryption keys + retention
metadata fields ship in MVP). The retention/crypto-shred engine is a Production Implementation; the
exact legal durations/policy are validated with counsel; an automated rights portal is Future.

## Context — the genuine tension
The DPDP Act grants data principals rights to **correction and erasure**. Indian medical-record rules
(clinical establishment regulations, professional/medico-legal requirements) **require retaining**
records for defined minimum periods. A patient's erasure request therefore cannot simply delete a
record that law requires the provider to keep.

The **engineering architecture can and should be finalized now.** Only the **exact retention durations
and the erasure-during-retention policy** depend on legal/compliance advisors — these are parameters,
not architecture, and are deferred for legal validation. We do not pretend the legal decisions are made.

## Decision — engineering architecture (finalized)
A **retention-policy engine + crypto-shredding**, parameterized by an externally-supplied policy:
- Each record class has a **configurable retention rule** (duration, legal basis, jurisdiction) loaded
  from policy configuration — the engine does not hard-code durations.
- PHI is encrypted with **per-record (or per-subject) keys** (Structural Foundation; ships in MVP).
- An erasure request is recorded and honored by **destroying keys** ("crypto-shredding") once the
  retention window expires — rendering data unrecoverable without racing physical deletion.
- Before window expiry, an erasure request is logged, acknowledged, and the record is
  restricted/locked from non-mandatory processing, with a lawful-basis explanation to the data principal.
- All actions are audited (ADR 0005) and linked to consent/withdrawal (ADR 0004).

### What ships in MVP (Structural Foundation)
Per-record encryption keys and the retention-metadata fields on the schema, plus the policy-config
seam. The engine and real durations are activated in the Production phase.

## Deferred for legal validation (parameters, gathered in parallel)
1. **Retention durations by record type** (OPD notes, lab, imaging, prescriptions, discharge summaries,
   minors' records, medico-legal cases — some with longer/indefinite holds).
2. **Erasure-during-retention policy**: restrict-and-defer (recommended) vs refuse-with-reason.
3. **Minors' data** handling under DPDP's stricter child-data rules + verifiable parental consent.
4. **Data Fiduciary / Significant Data Fiduciary** classification (triggers DPO, DPIA, independent audit).

These can be gathered with counsel in parallel with the MVP build; because the schema reserves the
fields and the engine reads policy from config, finalizing them later requires **no re-architecture**.
